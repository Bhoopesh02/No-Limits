## Brand & Style

This design system channels the raw, high-octane violence of extreme off-road rally racing across brutal terrain: loose shale, baked mud, sheer drops, and redline rev limiters. It merges rugged industrial utility with extreme telemetry and motorsport instrumentation.

The emotional profile targets intense focus, visceral feedback, and machine reliability. Key stylistic pillars include:

- **Mechanical Brutalism & Industrial Tactility:** High-contrast structural framing, visible panel assemblies, precision chamfers, mechanical bolt/rivet accents, and hazard-grade markings.
- **Off-Road Telemetry:** Data-dense displays, high-legibility dials, bold kinetic tracking, and technical readouts reminiscent of rally co-driver pacing tablets and roll-cage dash monitors.
- **Angular Cuts & Hazard Notches:** Diagonal 45-degree chamfers on corners, chevron directional indicators, and diagonal warning stripes that replace conventional soft curves with aggressive structural silhouettes.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in volcanic volcanic grit, baked dust, and high-visibility rally safety gear.

### Core Architecture

- **Base Canvas:** `#0E0C0A` (Warm, charcoal near-black)
- **Tonal Layers & Panels:**
  - `Surface Tier 1`: `#171411` (Deep chassis plate)
  - `Surface Tier 2`: `#211C17` (Raised pod / module mount)
  - `Surface Tier 3`: `#2B241D` (Interactive surface / elevated telemetry card)
- **Frame & Seam Borders:** `#3A3128` (Treated steel seam lines, 1px-2px rigid outlines)

### Accents & Kinetic Signals

- **Primary Accent (`#FF7A1A` - Burnt Orange):** Used for critical interactive triggers, primary velocity metrics, active state borders, and aggressive focal points.
- **Secondary Accent (`#FFB020` - Dusk Amber):** Secondary interactive components, lap delta callouts, warning indicators, and auxiliary gauges.

### Functional Feedback

- **Success (`#3DDC84`):** Checkpoints cleared, optimal rev ranges, green sector status.
- **Warning (`#FFC933`):** Traction loss warning, fluid heat alerts, marginal split times.
- **Danger (`#FF4D5E`):** Redline cutoffs, mechanical failure, terminal damage alerts.

### Typographic Contrasts

- **Primary Text:** `#F5EFE6` (Raw bone white, maximum contrast against deep soil bases)
- **Secondary Text:** `#B8AC9B` (Oxidized titanium, auxiliary labels and telemetry keys)
- **Disabled / Structural Text:** `#6B6153` (Etched carbon, inactive triggers, mechanical housing IDs)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces immediate legibility through extreme tension between mechanical speed and technical clarity:

- **Speed / Kinetic Displays (`Chakra Petch`):** Set in bold italic uppercase. Strictly assigned to primary impact copy, split timers, stage titles, and interactive trigger elements. Tabular lining numbers are enforced to prevent jitter during real-time telemetry shifts.
- **Technical Context & Telemetry Labels (`Barlow Condensed`):** Set in uppercase with wide tracking (`0.12em` to `0.18em`). This delivers rapid scanning capability under vibrating, high-stress conditions.
- **Descriptive Body (`Barlow`):** Provides grounded readability with subtle letter spacing, keeping stage briefing logs and mechanical diagnostics legible without distraction.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a rigid, industrial 12-column grid system modeled after physical cockpit instrument racks and tactical dashboard pods:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` outer canvas margin, `1.5rem` gutters. Modular blocks group related metrics into armored modules.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Lateral telemetry collapses into stacked control bands.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column compact layout, `1rem` margin, `0.75rem` gutter. Critical drive gauges and timing deltas take absolute full-width priority; secondary analytics convert into swipeable horizontal drawers.
- **Component Rhythm:** Interior padding obeys a dense, tool-grade cadence. Containers prioritize horizontal sweep over tall vertical padding to preserve cockpit vertical real estate.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, floating cloud-like drop shadows. Depth is purely mechanical and physical, achieved through stamped chassis plates, inset bevels, and directional luminescent spill:

- **Base Plate (Elevation 0):** `#0E0C0A` with subtle grit or carbon-grain structural texture.
- **Recessed / Engraved Cutouts (Elevation -1):** Inset borders using `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)` with a top border highlight of `#211C17` and bottom edge `#000000`. Used for data wells, gauge slots, and progress grooves.
- **Mounted Plates (Elevation 1):** `#171411` surface bound by a crisp 1px stroke of `#3A3128`. Hard cut 45-degree chamfers at opposing corners.
- **Raised Pods (Elevation 2):** `#211C17` surface, bordered with `#3A3128`. Hard drop projection: `4px 4px 0px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.95)` (tactile mechanical offset, zero blur).
- **Active Hotspot / Overdrive Glow:** Critical active components emit a directional amber/orange spill: `0 0 16px rgba(255, 122, 26, 0.35)`, layered over the 1px high-vis orange border (`#FF7A1A`).

## Shapes

Corner radii are strictly zero. The shape language rejects softened organic curves in favor of industrial hard angles:

- **Chamfered Cuts:** Primary components (cards, primary buttons, badges) employ angled corner cuts (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) at 8px to 14px sizes, mimicking laser-cut alloy chassis parts.
- **Hazard Notches:** Modular panels feature symmetrical triangular notches or 45-degree cutouts along secondary edges to evoke lock-pin slots and roll-cage bracket points.
- **Rivet Accents:** 4px mechanical bolt indicators (`#3A3128` circular deboss with a `#171411` core) are placed at the structural corners of primary dashboard cards.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary (Ignition):** Background `#FF7A1A`, text `#0E0C0A`, heavy bold italic `Chakra Petch`. Dual 10px chamfered corners on top-right and bottom-left. Inset top edge highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`). Hover triggers full luminescence with a secondary dusk amber (`#FFB020`) underline shift.
- **Secondary (Reinforced Steel):** Background `#211C17`, border 1px solid `#3A3128`, text `#F5EFE6`. Top-right chamfer. Hover state triggers a `#FF7A1A` border and `rgba(255, 122, 26, 0.1)` surface fill.
- **Danger / Kill-Switch:** Solid `#FF4D5E` with alternating black diagonal hazard striping along the left 12px anchor margin.

### Cards & Panels

- Constructed from `#171411` with a 1px `#3A3128` perimeter frame.
- Header bars feature a distinct recessed background `#211C17`, with integrated telemetry labels (`Barlow Condensed`, tracked wide).
- Corner rivets placed at `8px` offset in all non-chamfered corners.

### Chips & Badges

- Pill shapes are forbidden. Rectangular or single-chamfered tags with strict 1px framing.
- Format: `[STATUS // CODE]` using `label-sm` typography.
- Active stage chips use `#FF7A1A` text with 15% opacity orange backgrounds.

### Form Inputs

- Background `#171411`, recessed inset shadow, 1px perimeter of `#3A3128`.
- Focus state instantly snaps border to `#FF7A1A` with a micro diagonal-notch indicator appearing in the top-right corner.
- Monospaced numerals for numeric values; placeholders rendered in `#6B6153`.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches

- Checkboxes are squared alloy plates (18x18px) with a heavy `#3A3128` border. Checked state renders a high-contrast `#FF7A1A` solid diamond or chevron notch.
- Toggles utilize a sliding mechanical rocker switch aesthetic: 0px border radius, high-contrast `#0E0C0A` slider trench, and an illuminated `#FF7A1A` block actuator.

### Racing Telemetry Dials & Segment Bars

- Linear rev and boost gauges utilize segmented bar charts with 2px gap separators rather than smooth curves.
- Danger redline limits transition dynamically into pulsating `#FF4D5E` alerts.
